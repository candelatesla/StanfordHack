# Implementation and Onboarding

Typical implementation takes 4 to 6 weeks: week 1 connects data sources, weeks 2 to 3 build the first governed metrics and dashboards, and weeks 4 to 6 roll out to users with training. Most customers ship their first production dashboard within 2 weeks.

The one-time implementation fee is $15,000. It can be waived without approval on a 2-year or longer term; waiving it on a 1-year term requires VP Sales approval.

Enterprise includes a dedicated Customer Success Manager, live admin training, and access to Nimbus Academy self-paced courses. Additional on-site training days are available at $2,500 per day.
