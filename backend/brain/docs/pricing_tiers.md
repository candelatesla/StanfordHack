# Pricing Tiers

Nimbus Analytics sells three annual subscription tiers. **Starter** lists at $30,000 ARR and includes core analytics only. **Growth** lists at $80,000 ARR and adds SSO and API access. **Enterprise** lists at $150,000 ARR and adds a HIPAA Business Associate Agreement (BAA), EU data residency and a dedicated Customer Success Manager.

All prices are annual recurring revenue (ARR) before discounts. Upgrades between tiers are prorated. Features that are tier-gated cannot be sold à la carte on a lower tier; the customer must move up to the tier that includes them.
