# Data Ownership, Retention and Deletion

Customers own all of their data. Nimbus processes it only to provide the service and never sells it. Customer data is never used to train AI models.

Customers can export their dashboards, metrics definitions and query results at any time. After termination, customer data is deleted within 30 days, and backups age out within 35 days. A deletion certificate is available on request.

Custom retention periods shorter than the defaults are available on Enterprise. Longer retention commitments or legal holds should be routed to Legal.
