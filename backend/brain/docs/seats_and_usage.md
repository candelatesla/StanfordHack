# Seats and Usage

Viewers are unlimited and free on every tier. Creator seats (people who build dashboards and metrics) are included: 5 on Starter, 20 on Growth and 50 on Enterprise. Additional creator seats cost $600 per seat per year.

Usage is reviewed annually in a true-up. Nimbus does not charge overages mid-term or throttle usage; extra seats added mid-year are prorated.

Customers expecting large growth can pre-purchase creator seats in blocks of 25 at a 10% discount on the seat price.
