# HIPAA and Business Associate Agreements

Nimbus Analytics signs HIPAA Business Associate Agreements (BAA) with healthcare customers. The BAA is available on the **Enterprise tier only**. Customers on Starter or Growth who need to process protected health information (PHI) must upgrade to Enterprise.

Our BAA uses the standard Nimbus template, reviewed by Legal. PHI is encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.2+), and access is logged and audited.
