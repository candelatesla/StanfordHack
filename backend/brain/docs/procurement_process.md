# Procurement and Vendor Onboarding

Nimbus can be paid by ACH, wire, or purchase order; credit card payment is available for annual contracts under $50,000. Nimbus will fill in vendor registration forms and provide a W-9 and bank letter on request.

Nimbus carries $5M cyber liability insurance and $2M general liability insurance; certificates of insurance are available on request. The standard paper is the Nimbus MSA; reviewing a customer's own paper adds two to four weeks and must go through Legal.
