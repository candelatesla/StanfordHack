# Discount Policy

Reps may discount up to 10% on a 1-year term without approval. Multi-year commitments unlock larger pre-approved discounts: up to 12% for a 2-year term and up to 15% for a 3-year term. These caps are pre-approved and the rep can offer them on the call.

Any discount above 15% requires VP Sales approval, and any discount above 25% requires CFO approval. When a customer asks for more than the pre-approved cap, the recommended counteroffer is the maximum pre-approved discount for their term length, optionally paired with a longer commitment.
