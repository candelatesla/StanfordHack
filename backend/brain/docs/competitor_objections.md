# Competitor Objections

Against legacy BI tools, emphasize time-to-value: most Nimbus customers ship their first dashboard in under a week. Against warehouse-native tools, emphasize governed metrics and the healthcare compliance story (HIPAA BAA on Enterprise).

Do not disparage competitors by name, and do not match competitor pricing beyond the discount policy caps.

When a customer is comparing Nimbus with a lower-cost tool, ask which features they actually need and point out total cost: separate tools for governance, AI and embedding, plus implementation time. When the customer mentions a competitor's quote, ask to see it and compare like-for-like seats and features before discussing price.
