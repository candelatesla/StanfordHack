# Liability and Indemnity

The standard limitation of liability is capped at 1x the fees paid in the prior 12 months. Reps may agree to raise the cap up to 2x fees without approval. Any liability cap above 2x fees (for example 3x or uncapped) must be escalated to Legal.

Nimbus provides a standard IP infringement indemnity. Termination for convenience is not offered; customers may terminate for material breach with a 30-day cure period.
