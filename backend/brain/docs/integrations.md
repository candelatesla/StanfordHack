# Integrations

Nimbus connects natively to Snowflake, BigQuery, Redshift, Databricks, Postgres and SQL Server, and to business apps including Salesforce, HubSpot, NetSuite, Zendesk and Google Analytics. There are 50+ connectors in total, and new connectors are added monthly.

Nimbus works alongside dbt: it reads dbt models and metrics directly. Identity: SAML SSO with Okta, Azure AD and Google Workspace on Growth and Enterprise; SCIM user provisioning on Enterprise only.

The REST API is available on Growth and Enterprise with a limit of 1,000 requests per minute. Dashboards can be embedded in internal tools, and alerts can be sent to Slack, Microsoft Teams and email.
