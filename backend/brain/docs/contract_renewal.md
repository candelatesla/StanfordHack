# Contract Terms and Renewals

Subscriptions auto-renew for 12 months unless either party gives 60 days' written notice. Multi-year deals lock the price for the full committed term.

At renewal, standard price increases are up to 7%. Reps may agree to cap renewal increases at 5% without approval; a cap below 5% or a multi-year price freeze beyond the committed term requires VP Sales approval.

Start dates can be set up to 90 days after signature without approval. Mid-term upgrades to a higher tier are prorated; downgrades take effect only at renewal.
