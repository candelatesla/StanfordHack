# Pilots and Trials

Nimbus does not offer free trials on Enterprise. Instead, reps can offer a paid pilot of up to 30 days, scoped to one team and up to three data sources. The full pilot fee is credited toward the first-year contract if the customer signs within 30 days of the pilot ending.

Pilots longer than 30 days, or free pilots, require VP Sales approval. Every pilot must have written success criteria agreed before kickoff, a named executive sponsor, and a scheduled decision meeting.
