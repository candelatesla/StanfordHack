# Objection: Price Too High

When a customer says Nimbus is too expensive, first ask what they are comparing against: another vendor's quote, last year's budget, or the cost of building in-house. Most "too expensive" objections are really about unclear value, so anchor on outcomes before touching price.

Use the ROI talk track: Nimbus customers typically retire two to three legacy reporting tools and save 10 to 15 analyst hours per week, which usually covers the subscription within the first year. Offer to build a simple ROI model with their numbers.

If price is still the blocker, trade, don't give: a multi-year term unlocks up to 15% pre-approved, and the implementation fee can be waived on a 2-year or longer term. Never drop price without getting something back, such as a longer term, a case study, or faster signature.
