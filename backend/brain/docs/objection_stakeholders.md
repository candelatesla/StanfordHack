# Objection: Need Other Stakeholders

When the customer says they need sign-off from IT, Security, Legal or the CFO, ask who exactly is involved, what each person cares about, and whether you can join that conversation. Offer the right asset for each stakeholder.

For Security: the SOC 2 Type II report, completed security questionnaire and architecture overview. For Legal: the standard MSA, DPA and BAA templates. For Finance: the ROI model and pricing summary. For IT: the integrations list and implementation plan.

A mutual action plan with named owners and dates is the recommended way to keep multi-stakeholder deals on track.
