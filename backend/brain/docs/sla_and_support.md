# SLA and Support

Enterprise includes a 99.9% uptime SLA with service credits, 24/7 support and a 1-hour response time for Severity 1 issues. Growth includes a 99.5% uptime SLA and business-hours support. Starter has best-effort support.

SLA commitments above 99.9% are not offered and must be escalated to the VP of Engineering.

Nimbus has maintained 99.95% uptime over the last 12 months; live and historical status is published on the status page. Service credits for missing the 99.9% SLA are 10% of the monthly fee per full percentage point below target, capped at 30%.
