# Payment Terms

Standard payment terms are Net 30 from invoice date, billed annually in advance. Net 60 payment terms are pre-approved for deals with $100,000 ARR or more; reps can agree to Net 60 on the call for qualifying deals.

Payment terms longer than Net 60 (for example Net 90) require Finance approval and should be escalated. Quarterly billing is available on request for Enterprise deals but also requires Finance sign-off.
