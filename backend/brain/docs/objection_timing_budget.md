# Objection: Timing and Budget

If the customer says "not right now" or "no budget until next quarter", ask what changes next quarter and what it costs them to wait. Quantify the cost of delay using their current manual reporting hours.

Options to keep momentum: a paid pilot now that is credited toward the contract, a signed agreement with a delayed start date of up to 90 days, or quarterly billing (requires Finance approval). Reps may offer a start date up to 90 days after signature without approval.

If they need to think it over, agree on a specific next step and date before ending the call, and offer to send a one-page summary they can forward internally.
