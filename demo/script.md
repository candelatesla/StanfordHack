# Golden Demo Script — Nimbus Analytics × Acme Health

Roles: **Driver** (runs DealBrain on the rep laptop), **Customer** (second laptop in the Meet, plays TTS or reads lines), **Presenter**.

Setup: rep laptop opens the Meet tab + DealBrain tab → **Start listening** → pick the Meet tab, tick **"Also share tab audio"** → allow mic. Click ↺ to reset the deal first.

| # | Customer says | Expected card | Rep action |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | "Thanks for making time. We've reviewed the Enterprise proposal." | (no card) | small talk |
| 1 | "Can you do 20% off if we sign for 3 years?" | COUNTER: 20% needs VP Sales; say 15% for 3 yrs | Say the line → **Accept** (ARR → $127.5K) |
| 2 | "We're in healthcare. Do you sign a HIPAA BAA?" | INFO: yes, Enterprise only (Compliance doc) | **Accept** (BAA → contract Exhibit C) |
| 3 | "We need net-60 payment terms." | APPROVED: deals ≥ $100K ARR | **Accept** (Net 30 → Net 60) |
| 4 | "Can you raise the liability cap to 3x fees?" | ESCALATE: exceeds 2x, Legal | **Escalate to Legal** |
| 5 | "Okay, that works for us. Send over the paperwork." | (no card) | **End call** → summary |

Fallback: if audio fails, click the numbered chips under the transcript (text mode). Same cards, same CRM updates.
